Stef's Bakery started as a small dream to bring authentic artisanal baking traditions to the vibrant streets of Amsterdam.

Located on the picturesque Nieuwe Spiegelstraat, we open our doors every morning at 09:00 to welcome locals, artists, and travelers looking for a genuinely comforting space and exceptional coffee.
