Our Story

For generations, our family-run business has dedicated itself to crafting exceptional food with uncompromising quality. Located right in the vibrant heart of Amsterdam at Nieuwe Spiegelstraat 62B, Stef's Bakery is a warm hub for locals, food lovers, and coffee enthusiasts.

From our signature *Bouks Blend* specialty coffee to our hearty gourmet sandwiches and freshly baked pastries, everything we do is rooted in a genuine passion for great flavors and community.
